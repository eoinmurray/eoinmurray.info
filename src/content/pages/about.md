# Eoin Murray

[me@eoinmurray.info](mailto:me@eoinmurray.info)

Cambridge, UK · Valencia, Spain · Remote engagements

I split my time between a PhD in Computational Neuroscience at Cambridge and independent AI product work.

I simulate gamma rhythms in spiking neural networks, studying their computational role and potential to improve robustness in neuromorphic hardware.

1. [Pinglab](https://pinglab.eoinmurray.info): my PhD research.
2. [SNNLab](https://snnlab.eoinmurray.info): my spiking neural network simulator.

Commercially, I build LLM products from initial research through to production, including the roadmap and team. I take one remote engagement at a time, currently leading a product toward telecom-scale deployment.

## Experience

- _Marie Curie Fellow, Computational Neuroscience PhD._ University of Cambridge, in the lab of Prof. Timothy O'Leary. Simulating gamma rhythms in spiking neural networks, investigating the computational role of gamma itself and prospective applications in neuromorphic hardware for robustness. **2026-2029**.
- _Independent AI engineer and product lead._ Originated and built an LLM voice and chat product that guides users through complex support workflows, combining conversational interaction with navigation of a structured support graph. Took the product from a year of solo R&D to a development team, bringing on two engineers and helping assemble an internal client team. Now leading QA and preparation for a telecom pilot, with a target deployment of 500,000–1 million users. **2024–now**.
- _Time off._ **2022-2024**.
- _Co-founder, CEO & founding CTO._ Took Kyso through Techstars and raised $1.4 million in venture funding. Built the initial product, hired and led an eight-person team, and recruited a CTO to take over technical leadership. Brought the enterprise data-science platform to pilot with a global pharmaceutical company. Company closed in 2023. **2017–2022**.
- _Co-founder & CTO._ Co-founded Rinocloud, a "Dropbox for scientists." Helped raise venture funding, set the product direction, built the platform, and brought in its first research users. Left after 18 months to start Kyso; Rinocloud was acquired in the following year. **2016–2017**.
- _Marie Curie Fellow and quantum-device researcher._ Employed by Toshiba Research Cambridge while completing an MPhil at Cambridge. Developed quantum photonic devices and began a PhD before leaving to co-found Rinocloud. **2015–2016**.
